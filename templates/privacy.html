Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only what we need to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Technical data — basic information such as browser type and access times, used to keep the service secure and working.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Store your expenses and generate summaries and breakdowns for you.
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the service.
  • Contact you about important changes to your account or these policies.

We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers and is accessible only to you through your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text. We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which it will be permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate, such as web fonts served by Google Fonts and hosting infrastructure. These providers may receive limited technical data (for example, your IP address) as part of delivering their service. We do not share your expense data with third parties except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
